## Tips for Google Play Console

1. **Update regularly** - Keep descriptions fresh
2. **Use keywords naturally** - Don't stuff keywords
3. **Highlight unique features** - What makes you different
4. **Include privacy policy link** - Required
5. **Test different versions** - See what converts better
6. **Use emojis sparingly** - They help but don't overdo it
7. **Keep it scannable** - Use bullet points and sections
