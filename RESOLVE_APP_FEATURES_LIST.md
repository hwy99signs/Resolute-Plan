## App Capabilities

Resolve is a comprehensive goal tracking and commitment management application that helps users:

1. **Set Goals** - Create unlimited Resolves with milestones
2. **Track Progress** - Real-time progress tracking and analytics
3. **Stay Motivated** - Achievements, streaks, and notifications
4. **Reflect** - Journal entries linked to Resolves
5. **Analyze** - Detailed insights and productivity patterns
6. **Share** - Export and share Resolves with others
7. **Organize** - Categories, templates, and reminders
8. **Customize** - Dark mode, language, and notification preferences
