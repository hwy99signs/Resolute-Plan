Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to Resolute Plan pro ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and services.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us, including:

  • Account Information: Email address, password, full name, and profile picture
  • Resolve Data: Your goals, commitments, milestones, deadlines, and progress tracking
  • Activity Data: Habit completions, streak information, and achievement records
  • Usage Data: How you interact with the app, features used, and time spent
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, and unique device identifiers

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and maintain our services, including goal tracking and progress monitoring
  • Send you notifications about milestones, reminders, and achievements
  • Personalize your experience and provide relevant content
  • Analyze usage patterns to improve our app and develop new features
  • Communicate with you about your account, updates, and support requests
  • Ensure security and prevent fraud or abuse

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is securely stored using Supabase, a trusted cloud database service. We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • Encryption of data in transit and at rest
  • Secure authentication and access controls
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Limited access to personal data on a need-to-know basis

While we strive to protect your information, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your information only in the following circumstances:

  • With your explicit consent
  • To comply with legal obligations or respond to lawful requests
  • To protect our rights, privacy, safety, or property
  • In connection with a business transfer or merger (with notice to you)
  • With service providers who assist us in operating our app (under strict confidentiality agreements)

6. Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to:

  • Access and review your personal data through the app
  • Update or correct your information in your profile settings
  • Delete your account and associated data (contact support for assistance)
  • Opt-out of non-essential notifications in your notification settings
  • Export your data in PDF format through the export feature
  • Request a copy of your data or its deletion at any time

7. Children's Privacy

Resolute Plan pro is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately so we can delete such information.

8. International Data Transfers
